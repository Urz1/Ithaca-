**Subject: Request for Institutional Support and Pilot Facilitation for the Ithaca Research Intelligence Platform**

Excellency,

Following our recent technical presentation to the Ministry of Innovation and Technology (MInT), the Ithaca engineering initiative respectfully requests institutional support under the **Startup Proclamation No. 1396/2025** to pilot **Ithaca**, our homegrown scientific intelligence and research argumentation platform.

Across academia, researchers struggle to track how scientific ideas evolve, spending months manually reviewing literature while isolated papers lead to redundant research. Ithaca solves this through a breakthrough **scientific argumentation graph**—using artificial intelligence to map how research claims connect across publications, verifying directly whether studies support, dispute, or extend existing findings. This capability provides immense international scientific value while directly accelerating Ethiopian graduate research, eliminating costly duplication, and keeping national research data secure within domestic boundaries.

To deploy this platform as a strategic asset for our research ecosystem, we respectfully request MInT’s support in facilitating:

1. **Academic Data Access (EthERNet / NADRE):** Administrative clearance to connect with the National Academic Digital Repository of Ethiopia (NADRE), allowing open-access Ethiopian theses and articles to be mapped into Ithaca's argumentation graph.
2. **University Pilot Endorsement:** An official Letter of Recommendation to introduce Ithaca to public universities (including AAU, AASTU, ASTU, and Jimma University) for a 6-month pilot with graduate researchers and faculty.
3. **Local Cloud & Computing Support (EAII & Ethio Telecom):** Facilitation to access domestic cloud servers and computing resources, ensuring the system runs securely on local infrastructure.

Our engineering team will take full responsibility for all technical setup, system integration, and user training throughout the pilot. We are readily available to provide a demonstration or any additional information at your convenience, and we respectfully request your guidance and favorable consideration.
