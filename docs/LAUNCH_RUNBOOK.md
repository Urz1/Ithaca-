# Controlled Launch Runbook

## Alpha

- Use synthetic and openly licensed paper sets.
- Exercise account recovery, tenant isolation, imports, graph exploration,
  evidence correction, exports, provider failure, and backup restore.
- Resolve all critical and high security findings.

## Private beta

- Recruit three to five research labs with distinct disciplines and workflows.
- Provide guided onboarding and a named support contact.
- Review activation, time to first graph, failed jobs, correction quality, and
  support burden weekly.
- Do not expand access while data loss, tenant isolation, or unreproducible
  exports remain unresolved.

## Design-partner release

- Expand to ten to twenty teams only after the private-beta workflow and service
  objectives are consistently met.
- Publish support expectations, privacy terms, subprocessors, backup posture,
  and known limitations.
- Use feature flags and organization cohorts for every material capability.

## Promotion criteria

- Teams repeatedly complete the end-to-end evidence-review workflow.
- Cross-tenant authorization tests pass.
- Production build, tests, migrations, dependency audit, and security checks
  pass from a clean checkout.
- Backup restoration and incident-response exercises meet documented targets.
- Support can diagnose requests and jobs using correlation IDs without direct
  production database edits.
