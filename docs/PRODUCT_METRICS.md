# Product Metrics

## North-star workflow

A research team creates a project, discovers or imports papers, reviews evidence
relationships collaboratively, resolves uncertain classifications, and exports
a reproducible evidence report.

## Funnel

1. Account created.
2. Organization created or joined.
3. First project created.
4. First search or import completed.
5. First graph becomes interactive.
6. First evidence relationship reviewed.
7. A collaborator contributes.
8. A report or graph revision is exported.

## Primary measures

- Activation: organizations reaching first interactive graph within one day.
- Time to value: median time from account creation to first reviewed evidence.
- Collaboration: weekly organizations with contributions from two or more users.
- Workflow completion: organizations exporting after evidence review.
- Retention: organizations repeating the workflow in weeks 2, 4, and 8.
- AI trust: correction rate, agreement rate, evidence-view rate, and unresolved
  low-confidence classifications.
- Reliability impact: workflows abandoned after provider, job, or browser errors.

## Privacy rules

- Use pseudonymous user identifiers.
- Do not capture email, names, paper abstracts, search text, chat prompts, or
  imported content in analytics.
- Organization administrators must be able to disable optional analytics.
- Retention and deletion must follow the same lifecycle as the account or
  organization that produced the event.
